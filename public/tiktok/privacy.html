Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

Carousel Uploader is a personal tool built by Alexi Canamo. Only my own TikTok accounts use it.

What it collects: my TikTok access token, plus my basic account info like my user ID, display name, and profile picture. That's what TikTok returns when I log in. It doesn't collect anything from other people.

How it's used: only to send my carousels to my TikTok drafts.

Where it's stored: on my own computer. Slides are also hosted at a temporary public link while TikTok downloads them, then removed.

Sharing: nothing is sold or shared, apart from TikTok receiving the slides and captions I send.

Removing access: I can revoke it anytime in my TikTok settings.
